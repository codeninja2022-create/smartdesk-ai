# Code of Conduct

This article outlines the Code of Conduct at NovaTech Solutions, detailing expectations for professional behavior, handling conflicts of interest, maintaining confidentiality, responsible use of company assets, and social media guidelines. It also explains the consequences of breaches to ensure a respectful and ethical workplace.

## Professional Behaviour

At NovaTech Solutions, all employees are expected to:

1. Treat colleagues, clients, and stakeholders with respect and courtesy.
2. Maintain honesty and integrity in all professional interactions.
3. Follow safety protocols and procedures to ensure a safe working environment.
4. Report unethical conduct or violations of the Code of Conduct to the appropriate channels.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of NovaTech Solutions. This includes:

1. Disclosing any potential conflicts to your manager or HR.
2. Not engaging in activities that could compromise your ability to perform your job duties objectively.
3. Avoiding relationships or investments that could influence your decision-making at work.

## Confidentiality

All employees are required to protect the confidentiality of:

1. Company information, client data, and proprietary materials.
2. Not sharing, copying, or distributing confidential documents or data without authorization.
3. Not discussing sensitive company matters with individuals outside the organization.
4. Not using company information for personal gain or to benefit a third party.

Breaches of confidentiality are treated as serious violations and may lead to immediate termination and legal action.

## Use of Company Assets

Employees are expected to:

1. Use company assets, including technology and resources, responsibly and for business purposes only.
2. Protect company property from theft, damage, or misuse.
3. Report any loss or damage of company assets to the IT Service Desk or relevant department immediately.

## Social Media

When using social media, employees should:

1. Be mindful of how their posts may reflect on NovaTech Solutions.
2. Avoid disclosing confidential company information or making negative comments about the company or its employees.
3. Clearly state that opinions expressed are their own and do not represent the views of NovaTech Solutions.

## Consequences of Breaches

Violations of the Code of Conduct can result in:

1. Disciplinary action, which may include termination of employment.
2. Legal action if the violation involves illegal activity or serious misconduct.
3. Reporting incidents to appropriate authorities if necessary.

The company will determine the appropriate level of discipline based on the severity and circumstances of the violation.
