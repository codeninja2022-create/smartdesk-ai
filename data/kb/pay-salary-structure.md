# Salary Structure Explained

This article explains the salary structure at NovaTech Solutions, detailing the components of the Cost to Company (CTC), including basic salary, HRA, special allowance, provident fund (PF), gratuity, and performance bonus. It also clarifies the difference between gross pay and net pay.

## Understanding CTC Components

The Cost to Company (CTC) is the total amount that NovaTech Solutions spends on an employee in a year. Here are the main components of the CTC:

1. **Basic Salary**: This is 40% of your CTC. It forms the foundational part of your salary and is used to calculate other components like HRA and PF.
2. **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: HRA is 50% of your basic salary (for employees living in metro cities) and is provided to help cover housing costs.
3. **Special Allowance**: This is the remaining portion of your CTC after accounting for the basic salary, HRA, PF, gratuity, and bonus. It is flexible and can be used for various purposes.
4. **Provident Fund (PF)**: The employer contributes 12% of your basic salary towards the PF, which is a retirement savings scheme. This amount is part of your CTC but is not part of your take-home pay.
5. **Gratuity**: Gratuity is calculated as per the statute and is paid to employees who have completed at least five years of service. It is also included in the CTC.
6. **Performance Bonus**: Employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 10% of their CTC, which is based on individual and company performance.

## Gross Pay vs. Net Pay

Understanding the difference between gross pay and net pay is important for managing your finances:

1. **Gross Pay**: This is the total earnings before any deductions. It includes your basic salary, HRA, special allowance, PF contribution, gratuity, and performance bonus.
2. **Net Pay**: This is the amount you take home after all deductions, such as income tax and any other applicable deductions. Net pay is what you will see on your payslip in PeopleHub.
