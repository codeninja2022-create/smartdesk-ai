# Data Backup and File Recovery

This article provides guidance on backing up data using OneDrive, restoring deleted files, accessing previous versions, and understanding retention policies. It also clarifies what data is not backed up by OneDrive.

## Backing Up Data Using OneDrive

To ensure your Desktop and Documents folders are backed up using OneDrive, follow these steps:

1. **Open OneDrive**: Click on the OneDrive cloud icon in your system tray (Windows) or menu bar (macOS).
2. **Settings**: Right-click the OneDrive icon and select **Settings**.
3. **Backup Tab**: Navigate to the **Backup** tab.
4. **Manage Backup**: Click on **Manage backup**.
5. **Select Folders**: Ensure that both **Desktop** and **Documents** are checked.
6. **Start Backup**: Click on **Start backup** to initiate the backup process. 
7. **Confirmation**: Wait for the confirmation that your files are being backed up.

## Restoring Deleted Files and Previous Versions

If you accidentally delete files or need to restore previous versions, use the following steps:

### To Restore Deleted Files:
1. **Open OneDrive**: Go to the OneDrive website or open the OneDrive app.
2. **Recycle Bin**: Click on the **Recycle Bin** on the left sidebar.
3. **Select Files**: Find the files you want to restore, select them.
4. **Restore**: Click on the **Restore** button to recover the selected files.

### To Restore Previous Versions:
1. **Open OneDrive**: Navigate to the folder containing the file.
2. **Select File**: Right-click on the file you want to restore.
3. **Version History**: Click on **Version history**.
4. **Select Version**: Choose the version you want to restore and click **Restore**.

## Retention Policies

OneDrive retains deleted files in the Recycle Bin for 30 days. After this period, files are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Ensure to regularly check your Recycle Bin for any files you may need to restore.

## What is Not Backed Up

Be aware that certain data is not backed up by OneDrive, including:
- Files stored in the **local C drive** that are not in the Desktop or Documents folders.
- Files larger than 250 GB.
- Files that are not synced to OneDrive due to sync errors or settings.
- Temporary files and system files.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I check if my OneDrive backup is complete?

To verify if your OneDrive backup is complete, check the OneDrive app or website for the status of your files. Ensure that the sync status shows 'Up to date' and that all files in your Desktop and Documents folders are present.

### What should I do if I can't find my backed-up files?

If you can't find your backed-up files, first check the OneDrive Recycle Bin. If they are not there, ensure that your files are located in the correct folders (Desktop or Documents) and check for any sync issues.

### Can I restore files older than 30 days from OneDrive?

No, OneDrive permanently deletes files from the Recycle Bin after 30 days. If you need to recover files older than this, they cannot be restored.

### How do I set up automated backups for my files?

OneDrive automatically backs up files in your Desktop and Documents folders. Ensure that the backup settings are configured correctly in the OneDrive app as outlined in the 'Backing Up Data Using OneDrive' section.

### What if my OneDrive backup fails?

If your OneDrive backup fails, check your internet connection and ensure that OneDrive is running. You can also review the sync status in the app for any errors and troubleshoot accordingly.
