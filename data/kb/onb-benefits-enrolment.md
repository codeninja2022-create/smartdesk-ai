# Benefits Enrolment for New Joiners

This article outlines the process for new joiners at NovaTech Solutions to enroll in benefits, including health insurance, and how to manage dependants and nominations for PF and gratuity. It also explains the implications of missing the enrollment window.

## Health Insurance Enrollment

As a new joiner, you must enroll in the group health insurance plan within 30 days of your joining date. Here’s how to do it:

1. Log in to PeopleHub using your Okta credentials.
2. Navigate to the 'Benefits' section.
3. Select 'Health Insurance' from the available options.
4. Complete the enrollment form, ensuring you provide all required information.
5. Submit the form for processing. You will receive confirmation once your enrollment is successful.

## Adding Dependants to Your Health Insurance

If you wish to add dependants (spouse and children) to your health insurance plan, you can do so during the initial enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying event (e.g., marriage, birth of a child). To add dependants:

1. Log in to PeopleHub.
2. Go to the 'Benefits' section.
3. Click on 'Health Insurance' and select 'Add Dependants'.
4. Fill in the required details for each dependant.
5. Submit the changes for approval.

## PF and Gratuity Nomination

It is important to nominate beneficiaries for your Provident Fund (PF) and gratuity. Here’s how:

1. Log in to PeopleHub.
2. Navigate to the 'Profile' section.
3. Select 'Nomination' from the options available.
4. Fill out the nomination forms for both PF and gratuity, providing the necessary details of your nominees.
5. Submit the forms for processing.

## Missing the Enrollment Window

If you miss the 30-day window for enrolling in health insurance or adding dependants, you will not be able to enroll until the next open enrollment period, which typically occurs once a year. However, you may still be able to enroll if you experience a qualifying life event. For assistance, please contact the HR Help Desk.

## Frequently asked questions

### What happens if I miss the deadline to enroll in health insurance?

If you miss the 30-day enrollment window, you will have to wait for the next open enrollment period to enroll in health insurance, unless you have a qualifying life event.

### Can I change my health insurance plan after I enroll?

Changes to your health insurance plan can typically only be made during the annual open enrollment period or if you experience a qualifying life event.

### How do I know if my dependant has been successfully added to my health insurance?

You will receive a confirmation notification in PeopleHub once your dependant has been successfully added to your health insurance.

### Is there a specific format for the PF and gratuity nomination?

There is no specific format required, but you must provide all necessary details of your nominees as requested in the PeopleHub nomination forms.

### What should I do if I have questions about my benefits enrollment?

If you have any questions or need assistance with your benefits enrollment, please contact the HR Help Desk via ServiceHub or email hr@novatech.example.
