# Expense Reimbursement

This article outlines the process for expense reimbursement at NovaTech Solutions, detailing what expenses are eligible for reimbursement, how to submit claims through PeopleHub, and the approval and payment timelines.

## Reimbursable Expenses

At NovaTech Solutions, the following expenses are typically eligible for reimbursement:
1. Business travel expenses (transportation, accommodation, meals).
2. Work-related supplies and materials.
3. Training and development costs (within the learning budget).
4. Any other expenses pre-approved by your manager.

Please ensure that all expenses are reasonable and necessary for your role.

## Submitting Expenses in PeopleHub

To submit your expense claims in PeopleHub, follow these steps:
1. Log in to PeopleHub (https://peoplehub.novatech.example).
2. Navigate to the 'Expenses' section.
3. Click on 'Submit New Expense'.
4. Fill in the required details for each expense, including the date, amount, and category.
5. Upload scanned copies or images of your receipts for each expense.
6. Review your submission for accuracy and click 'Submit'.

Ensure that all receipts are clear and legible to avoid delays in processing.

## Deadlines for Submission

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense. Claims submitted after this period may not be eligible for reimbursement.

## Approval Flow

Once submitted, your expense claim will go through the following approval process:
1. Your manager will review and approve the claim.
2. After manager approval, the Finance team will review the claim for compliance with company policies.
3. If approved by Finance, the claim will be processed for payment.

## Payment Timing

Approved expense reimbursements will be paid out in the next payroll run. Ensure that your claims are submitted and approved in time to be included in the upcoming payroll.

## Rejected Claims

If your expense claim is rejected:
1. You will receive a notification explaining the reason for rejection.
2. You can revise the claim based on the feedback and resubmit it for approval.
3. If you have questions about the rejection, please reach out to your manager or the HR Help Desk for clarification.
