# Email on Your Mobile Device

This article provides step-by-step instructions for setting up the Outlook mobile app for Microsoft 365 on iOS and Android devices, along with guidance on Intune app protection and troubleshooting sync issues.

## Setting Up Outlook Mobile for Microsoft 365

To set up your company email on the Outlook mobile app, follow these steps:

1. **Download the Outlook App**: 
   - For iOS: Go to the App Store, search for "Microsoft Outlook," and download the app.
   - For Android: Go to Google Play Store, search for "Microsoft Outlook," and download the app.

2. **Open the Outlook App**: Launch the app after installation.

3. **Add Your Account**: 
   - Tap on "Get Started" or "Add Account."
   - Enter your company email address (yourname@novatech.example) and tap "Continue."

4. **Sign In**: 
   - You will be redirected to the Okta login page. Enter your Okta credentials (username and password).
   - Complete the multi-factor authentication (MFA) if prompted (via Okta Verify or hardware key).

5. **Configure Settings**: 
   - Follow the prompts to allow notifications and access to your contacts and calendar.
   - Adjust any additional settings as per your preference.

6. **Finish Setup**: 
   - Once the setup is complete, you should see your inbox and can start sending and receiving emails.

## Intune App Protection

To ensure your Outlook app is protected under Intune, follow these steps:

1. **Install Company Portal**: Make sure you have the Microsoft Intune Company Portal app installed on your device.
   - For iOS: Download from the App Store.
   - For Android: Download from Google Play Store.

2. **Sign In to Company Portal**: Open the Company Portal app and sign in with your company credentials.

3. **Enroll Your Device**: Follow the prompts to enroll your device in Intune. This may include setting up a device passcode and agreeing to company policies.

4. **Access Outlook**: Once your device is enrolled, open the Outlook app. You may be prompted to accept additional security policies.

## Troubleshooting Email Sync Issues

If you experience issues with email synchronization on your mobile device, try the following steps:

1. **Check Internet Connection**: Ensure your device is connected to the internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data).

2. **Refresh the App**: 
   - Open the Outlook app and swipe down on the inbox to refresh.

3. **Check Account Settings**: 
   - Go to Settings in the Outlook app, select your account, and verify that your email address and password are correct.

4. **Re-sync Your Account**: 
   - In the account settings, look for an option to "Sync" or "Reset Account" and follow the prompts.

5. **Reinstall the App**: If issues persist, uninstall and reinstall the Outlook app from your device.

6. **Contact IT Support**: If you are still having trouble, please reach out to the IT Service Desk for further assistance.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I set up my company email on a new mobile device?

Follow the steps outlined in the 'Setting Up Outlook Mobile for Microsoft 365' section above.

### What should I do if I can't sync my emails on the Outlook app?

Refer to the 'Troubleshooting Email Sync Issues' section for steps to resolve sync problems.

### Is there any app protection for Outlook on my mobile device?

Yes, ensure you have the Microsoft Intune Company Portal app installed and your device enrolled for app protection.

### Can I access my calendar and contacts through the Outlook mobile app?

Yes, the Outlook app allows you to access your calendar and contacts once you have logged in with your company email.

### What if I encounter authentication errors while setting up my email?

Double-check your email address and password. If the issue persists, contact the IT Service Desk for assistance.
