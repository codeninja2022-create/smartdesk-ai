# Probation Period

This article outlines the key aspects of the probation period at NovaTech Solutions, including its length, confirmation process, potential for extension, notice period, and leave policies during probation.

## Length of Probation

The probation period at NovaTech Solutions lasts for 6 months from the date of joining.

## Confirmation Process

1. At the end of the probation period, your manager will conduct a review to assess your performance.
2. If your performance meets the expectations, you will be confirmed in your role.
3. Confirmation will be communicated formally to you.

## Extension of Probation

1. If your performance does not meet the required standards, your probation period may be extended.
2. The decision to extend probation will be made by your manager in consultation with HR.
3. You will be informed of the extension and the reasons for it.

## Notice Period During Probation

1. The notice period during the probation period is 30 days.
2. If you wish to resign during this time, you must provide written notice to your manager.

## Leave During Probation

1. You are entitled to casual leave and sick leave during your probation period as per company policy.
2. Casual leave: 12 days per year (no carry-forward, no encashment, maximum 3 consecutive days).
3. Sick leave: 8 days per year (medical certificate required for more than 2 consecutive days, no encashment).
4. Leave must be applied for and approved in PeopleHub.
