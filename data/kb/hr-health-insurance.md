# Health Insurance and Benefits

This article provides an overview of the health insurance and benefits offered by NovaTech Solutions, including details on group health insurance coverage, term life insurance, the claims process, cashless hospital services, and how to add a new dependant to your policy.

## Group Health Insurance Coverage

NovaTech Solutions provides group health insurance coverage of INR 5 lakh under a family floater plan. This coverage includes:
1. Employee
2. Spouse
3. Two children
4. Parents (optional at employee cost)

To enroll in the group health insurance plan, employees must complete the enrollment process within 30 days of joining the company through PeopleHub Benefits.

## Group Term Life Insurance

In addition to health insurance, employees are covered by a group term life insurance policy that provides coverage of three times the employee's annual Cost to Company (CTC). This benefit is automatically included for all employees.

## Making a Claim

To make a claim under the group health insurance policy, follow these steps:
1. **Notify the Insurance Provider**: Contact the insurance provider as soon as possible to inform them of the claim.
2. **Gather Required Documents**: Collect necessary documents such as:
   - Medical bills
   - Discharge summary
   - Any other relevant medical records
3. **Submit Claim Form**: Fill out the claim form provided by the insurance provider and submit it along with the gathered documents.
4. **Follow Up**: Keep track of your claim status by contacting the insurance provider directly.

## Cashless Hospitals

NovaTech Solutions has tie-ups with several hospitals that offer cashless treatment. To avail of cashless hospitalization:
1. **Check Network Hospitals**: Verify if the hospital is part of the insurance provider's network.
2. **Pre-Authorization**: Obtain pre-authorization from the insurance provider before admission. This can usually be done by the hospital's insurance desk.
3. **Present Required Documents**: Provide necessary documents such as your employee ID, insurance card, and any other required identification at the hospital.

## Adding a New Dependant

To add a new dependant to your group health insurance policy, please follow these steps:
1. **Log in to PeopleHub**: Access your account on PeopleHub.
2. **Navigate to Benefits**: Go to the 'Benefits' section.
3. **Select Health Insurance**: Click on the health insurance option.
4. **Add Dependant**: Follow the prompts to add your new dependant's details.
5. **Submit for Approval**: After entering the information, submit it for HR approval. 

Please ensure that you complete this process within 30 days of the event (e.g., marriage, birth of a child) to ensure coverage.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is covered under the group health insurance policy?

The group health insurance policy covers the employee, spouse, two children, and optionally, parents at the employee's cost, with a total coverage of INR 5 lakh.

### How do I make a claim for medical expenses?

To make a claim, notify the insurance provider, gather required documents, submit the claim form along with the documents, and follow up on the claim status.

### What should I do if I need treatment at a hospital?

If you need treatment, check if the hospital is part of the insurance provider's network, obtain pre-authorization, and present the required documents at the hospital.

### How can I add a new dependant to my health insurance?

To add a new dependant, log in to PeopleHub, navigate to the Benefits section, select health insurance, enter the dependant's details, and submit for HR approval.

### Is there a time limit for enrolling in health insurance after joining?

Yes, you must enroll in the group health insurance within 30 days of joining the company.
