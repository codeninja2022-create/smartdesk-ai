# SmartDesk AI

NovaTech's IT and HR help desk assistant.

- **Ask a question.** Answers come only from NovaTech's knowledge base and cite
  their sources; click a source to read the cited section in the side panel.
  When the knowledge base has no answer, SmartDesk says so and offers a ticket.
- **Raise a ticket.** SmartDesk collects the details and shows the full ticket
  before anything is sent to Jira. Use **Approve**, **Edit** or **Cancel**;
  no ticket is created without your approval of its final details.
- **Check your tickets.** See status and support-team updates for the tickets
  linked to your email.

Your email is a demo identifier, not a login: it is locked to the chat once
given. Start a **New Chat** to use a different one. Conversations are stored
locally in `data/smartdesk.db`.
