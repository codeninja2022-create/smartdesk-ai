# Public and Floating Holidays

This article outlines the fixed public holidays and floating holidays at NovaTech Solutions, including how to select floating holidays, policies regarding working on holidays, and compensatory off (comp-off) for approved work during these times.

## Public Holidays

At NovaTech Solutions, employees are entitled to 12 fixed public holidays each year. These holidays are predetermined and communicated at the beginning of the year. Employees are encouraged to plan their leave around these holidays to maximize their time off.

## Floating Holidays

In addition to the fixed public holidays, employees can choose 2 floating holidays from a published list each year. To select your floating holidays:

1. Review the published list of floating holidays provided by HR.
2. Log in to PeopleHub.
3. Navigate to the Leave section.
4. Select your preferred floating holidays and submit your request for approval.
5. Ensure your manager approves your floating holiday requests.

## Working on a Holiday

If you are required to work on a public holiday, please note the following:

1. You must obtain prior approval from your manager to work on a holiday.
2. Overtime for work performed on public holidays is calculated separately and will be compensated according to company policy.
3. Ensure you document your hours worked and submit them for approval to receive the appropriate compensation.

## Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)

If you work on a public holiday or a weekend, you may be eligible for compensatory off (comp-off). Here’s how it works:

1. Comp-off is granted for approved work on weekends or public holidays.
2. You must submit a request for comp-off through PeopleHub after your work has been approved.
3. Comp-off must be utilized within 60 days of the holiday worked.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do public holidays have a different overtime calculation?

Yes, overtime for work performed on public holidays is calculated separately and requires prior approval, according to company policy.

### Do I need approval to work overtime on weekends or public holidays?

Yes, working overtime on weekends or public holidays requires prior approval according to company policy.

### Can I carry forward unused floating holidays to the next year?

No, unused floating holidays cannot be carried forward to the next year; they must be used within the calendar year.

### What happens if a public holiday falls on a weekend?

If a public holiday falls on a weekend, it is typically not replaced with an additional day off; however, employees should check the company policy for specific guidance.

### How do I report my hours worked on a public holiday?

You should document your hours worked and submit them through the appropriate channels for approval to ensure proper compensation.
