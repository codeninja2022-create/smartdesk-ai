# Password Reset

This article provides guidance on how to reset your password using the self-service password portal at NovaTech Solutions, outlines the password rules and expiry policies, and offers troubleshooting steps if you encounter issues with the portal.

## Self-Service Password Reset via the Password Portal

To reset your password, follow these steps:

1. Go to the Password Portal: [https://passwords.novatech.example](https://passwords.novatech.example).
2. Click on the 'Forgot Password?' link.
3. Enter your registered email address and submit the request.
4. Check your email for a password reset link. If you don't see it, check your spam/junk folder.
5. Click the link in the email to set a new password.
6. Follow the prompts to create your new password, ensuring it meets the password rules outlined below.

## Password Rules and Expiry

When creating a new password, please adhere to the following rules:
- Minimum length: 8 characters
- Must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character (e.g., !@#$%^&*).
- No part of your username or easily guessable information (like your name or birthdate).

Passwords expire every 90 days. You will receive a reminder email 7 days before your password expires, prompting you to update it.

## What to Do If the Portal Fails

If you encounter issues with the password portal, such as links not generating or emails not being received, please follow these troubleshooting steps:

1. Ensure you are using the correct email address associated with your account.
2. Check your spam/junk folder for the password reset email.
3. Clear your browser cache or try using a different browser.
4. If the problem persists, contact the IT Service Desk:
   - Via ServiceHub: [ServiceHub](https://servicehub.novatech.example)
   - Email: itsupport@novatech.example
   - Phone: ext. 2020 (available 08:00-20:00 IST, Monday to Friday).
   - For urgent issues (P1 outages), call ext. 2020, available 24x7.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I do if I forget my password?

You can reset your password using the self-service password portal at [https://passwords.novatech.example](https://passwords.novatech.example). Follow the instructions for 'Forgot Password?' to receive a reset link.

### How often do I need to change my password?

Passwords must be changed every 90 days. You will receive a reminder email 7 days prior to the expiry.

### What are the requirements for creating a new password?

Your new password must be at least 8 characters long and include one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character. It should not contain easily guessable information.

### What should I do if I don't receive the password reset email?

Check your spam/junk folder. If you still don't receive it, ensure you entered the correct email address and try again. If the issue persists, contact the IT Service Desk for assistance.

### Who do I contact if I have trouble with the password portal?

If you experience issues with the password portal, please reach out to the IT Service Desk via ServiceHub, email itsupport@novatech.example, or call ext. 2020.
