# Account Lockout and Unlock

This article explains the reasons why employee accounts may become locked, the self-service options available for unlocking accounts, and guidance on when to contact the IT Service Desk for further assistance.

## Why Accounts Lock

Employee accounts may become locked for several reasons:

1. **Failed Login Attempts**: If you enter the wrong password multiple times (typically more than 5 attempts), your account will be temporarily locked as a security measure.
2. **Stale Credentials on Devices**: Sometimes, devices such as smartphones or tablets may retain outdated login credentials. If these devices attempt to log in with incorrect credentials, it can lead to account lockout.

## Self-Service Unlock Options

If you find yourself locked out of your account, you can attempt to unlock it using the self-service password reset option:

1. Go to the Password Portal: [Password Portal](https://passwords.novatech.example).
2. Click on the "Unlock Account" option.
3. Follow the prompts to verify your identity (you may need to use your Okta credentials).
4. Once verified, your account will be unlocked automatically.

## When to Call the Service Desk

If you are unable to unlock your account using the self-service options, or if you encounter issues such as:

- You are locked out after too many failed attempts and cannot reset your password on your own.
- You suspect that your account has been compromised or flagged for unusual activity.
- You have stale credentials on devices that you cannot resolve.

In these cases, please contact the IT Service Desk:
- **Email**: itsupport@novatech.example
- **Phone**: ext. 2020
- **Hours**: 08:00-20:00 IST Monday to Friday; for P1 outages, available 24x7.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I do if I'm locked out of my account and can't unlock it myself?

If you're unable to unlock your account using the self-service options, please contact the IT Service Desk for assistance.

### How can I prevent my account from getting locked?

To prevent account lockouts, ensure that you are using the correct password and update any saved credentials on your devices to avoid stale credentials.

### What happens if my account is locked due to too many failed login attempts?

Your account will be temporarily locked for security reasons. You can attempt to unlock it via the Password Portal or contact the IT Service Desk if you need further help.

### Can I unlock my account from any device?

Yes, as long as you have access to the Password Portal, you can unlock your account from any device with internet access.

### What are stale credentials and how do they affect my account?

Stale credentials are outdated login details saved on devices. If these devices attempt to log in with incorrect credentials, it can lead to your account being locked.
