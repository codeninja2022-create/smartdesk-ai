# Hybrid and Work-from-Home Policy

This article outlines the hybrid and work-from-home policy at NovaTech Solutions, detailing office days, anchor days, extra remote days, exceptions for fully remote roles, home-working expectations, and the internet allowance for hybrid employees.

## Hybrid Work Model Overview

At NovaTech Solutions, we embrace a hybrid work model that combines in-office and remote work to enhance flexibility and productivity. Here are the key components of our hybrid work policy:

1. **Office Days**: Employees are expected to work from the office three days a week.
2. **Anchor Days**: Tuesday to Thursday are designated as anchor days, where all employees should be present in the office.
3. **Extra Remote Days**: Employees may work remotely for up to 10 additional full days per quarter, subject to manager approval.
4. **Fully Remote Exceptions**: Fully remote roles are available only by exception and require approval from HR and the department head.

## Home-Working Expectations

When working from home, employees are expected to:

1. Be reachable via phone, email, and internal chat during core working hours (11:00-16:00 IST).
2. Attend all scheduled meetings, whether virtual or in-person, unless excused by their manager.
3. Proactively communicate progress, challenges, and support needs to their team and manager.
4. Use approved collaboration tools for all work-related communication and document sharing.

## Internet Allowance for Hybrid Employees

Hybrid employees are entitled to a monthly internet allowance of INR 1,500. This allowance is intended to support home working conditions and ensure reliable internet access for productivity.

## Policy Review and Exceptions

1. The hybrid work policy is subject to periodic review and may be amended as per business requirements.
2. Any exceptions to the remote work policy guidelines require written approval from management.

### How are exceptions to the remote work policy handled?

Any exceptions to the remote work policy guidelines require written approval from management. The policy is reviewed periodically and updated as needed to address changing requirements or circumstances.

### How often is the Work From Home (WFH) policy reviewed and can it be changed?

The Work From Home (WFH) policy is subject to periodic review and may be amended as per business requirements. This means the company regularly evaluates the policy and can make changes when necessary to align with organizational needs.

### How is employee performance monitored and reviewed in a remote or hybrid work setting?

Managers set clear deliverables and key performance indicators (KPIs) for each employee. Regular check-ins and performance reviews are conducted to assess output and address any concerns. Consistent underperformance or lack of communication may lead to a review of the employee's eligibility for work-from-home or hybrid arrangements.

### What are the communication and collaboration expectations for remote employees?

Remote employees are expected to be reachable via phone, email, and internal chat during working hours. Attendance at scheduled meetings, whether virtual or in-person, is mandatory unless excused by the manager. Employees should proactively communicate their progress, challenges, and support needs to their team and manager.

### What should I do if I am unable to attend a meeting while working from home?

If you are unable to attend a meeting while working from home, you should inform the relevant parties in advance. Providing prior intimation helps you stay compliant with the WFH policy.
