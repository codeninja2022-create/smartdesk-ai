# Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy

This article outlines the Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy at NovaTech Solutions, detailing prohibited payments, limits on gifts and hospitality, the process for declaring gifts, guidelines for dealing with government officials, and the procedure for reporting concerns. Compliance with this policy is crucial to maintaining the integrity and reputation of our company.

## Prohibited Payments

1. NovaTech Solutions strictly prohibits offering, giving, receiving, or soliciting any form of bribe or corrupt payment, whether directly or indirectly.
2. This includes payments intended to influence business decisions or gain unfair advantages in any business dealings.

## Gifts and Hospitality Limits

1. Employees may accept or offer gifts and hospitality only if they are reasonable, proportionate, and not intended to influence business decisions.
2. Gifts exceeding INR 5,000 in value must be reported to the Compliance Team for proper documentation.
3. Hospitality must be modest and appropriate to the occasion, and any expenses exceeding INR 10,000 must also be reported.

## Declaring Gifts

1. Employees must declare any gifts received or offered that exceed the specified value limits.
2. To declare a gift, complete the Gifts Declaration Form available on the HR portal and submit it to the Compliance Team within 5 working days of receipt or offer.

## Dealing with Government Officials

1. When interacting with government officials, employees must adhere to strict guidelines to avoid any appearance of impropriety.
2. Any gifts or hospitality offered to government officials must be pre-approved by the Compliance Team and must comply with local laws and regulations.

## Reporting Concerns

1. If you suspect a violation of the Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy, report your concerns immediately to the Compliance Team.
2. You can report concerns via email or through the designated reporting channel, ensuring confidentiality and protection against retaliation.
