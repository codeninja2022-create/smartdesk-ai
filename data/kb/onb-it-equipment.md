# IT Equipment Allocation for New Joiners

This article outlines the IT equipment allocation process for new joiners at NovaTech Solutions, detailing the laptop models provided based on role, the timing of equipment distribution, accessories included, the first login and setup process, and the policy for returning equipment upon employment termination.

## Laptop Models by Role

At NovaTech Solutions, the following laptop models are allocated based on the role of the employee:

1. **Engineering/Design Roles**: MacBook Pro 14"
2. **Other Roles**: Dell Latitude 7450

Each laptop is requested by the hiring manager 10 working days before the new joiner's start date to ensure availability.

## When You Get Your Equipment

On your first day, you will receive your laptop during the onboarding process at the Bellandur office reception. The laptop handover will occur alongside your HR induction, where you will also receive your ID badge and set up your Okta and MFA.

## Included Accessories

Along with your laptop, you will receive the following accessories:

- Charger
- Laptop bag
- Any additional peripherals as required for your role (e.g., mouse, keyboard)

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the company's policy on returning assets after employment ends?

The company's policy requires that all company-provided equipment and assets be returned in good condition upon termination of employment or when requested by the company. This must be done within the stipulated time frame.

### Do I need to return company equipment in a specific condition?

Yes, all company-provided equipment and assets must be returned in good condition, as specified by the company's policy.

### If I accidentally damage company equipment before returning it, what should I do?

If you accidentally damage company equipment, you should promptly inform your employer. The company may assess the damage and determine if repair or replacement costs will be deducted from your final settlement, as per company policy.

### What should I do for my first login and setup?

For your first login, use your Okta credentials to access your laptop and set up MFA via Okta Verify. Follow the prompts to complete the initial setup, including connecting to the corporate Wi-Fi and accessing internal systems.
