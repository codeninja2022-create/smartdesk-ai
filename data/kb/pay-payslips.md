# Payslips and Payroll Corrections

This article provides guidance on how to download your payslips from PeopleHub, understand the details presented in each line of the payslip, and report any discrepancies regarding salary or deductions. It also covers information on arrears and payroll corrections.

## Downloading Payslips from PeopleHub

1. Log in to PeopleHub at [https://peoplehub.novatech.example](https://peoplehub.novatech.example) using your Okta credentials.
2. Navigate to the ‘Payroll’ section from the main menu.
3. Click on ‘Payslips’ to view your available payslips.
4. Select the payslip for the relevant month.
5. Click on the ‘Download’ button to save the payslip as a PDF file on your device.

## Reading Your Payslip

Your payslip contains several key components:

1. **Employee Information**: Your name, employee ID, and designation.
2. **Pay Period**: The month for which the salary is being paid.
3. **Earnings**: This section lists your basic salary, allowances (HRA, special allowance), and any bonuses.
4. **Deductions**: This includes Employee Provident Fund (EPF), professional tax, and any other deductions applicable.
5. **Net Pay**: The final amount credited to your bank account after all deductions.
6. **Arrears**: Any pending payments from previous months, if applicable.

## Reporting an Incorrect Salary or Deduction

If you notice any discrepancies in your salary or deductions, follow these steps:

1. Gather supporting documents (e.g., previous payslips, offer letter).
2. Log in to PeopleHub and navigate to the ‘Payroll’ section.
3. Click on ‘Report Issue’ or ‘Payroll Correction’ option.
4. Fill in the details of the discrepancy, including the specific line item affected.
5. Attach any supporting documents.
6. Submit the request for review. You will receive a confirmation email with a ticket number.

## Arrears and Corrections

1. **Arrears**: If you have received arrears, it will be reflected in the earnings section of your payslip. This may occur due to delayed salary adjustments or corrections from previous months.
2. **Payroll Corrections**: If a correction is needed, it will be processed in the next payroll cycle. Ensure you report any discrepancies as soon as possible to avoid delays.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I access my payslips?

You can access your payslips by logging into PeopleHub and navigating to the ‘Payroll’ section, then selecting ‘Payslips’.

### What should I do if I find an error in my payslip?

If you find an error, gather supporting documents and report the issue through the ‘Report Issue’ option in PeopleHub.

### What is included in the deductions section of my payslip?

The deductions section typically includes Employee Provident Fund (EPF), professional tax, and any other applicable deductions.

### How are arrears reflected in my payslip?

Arrears will be shown in the earnings section of your payslip as additional payments from previous months.

### How long does it take to correct a payroll discrepancy?

Corrections are usually processed in the next payroll cycle, so it is important to report discrepancies promptly.
