4. **12:00 - 13:00**: Introduction to Your Buddy

6. **14:00 - 16:00**: Team Introduction and Overview of Your Role
7. **16:00 - 17:00**: Q&A Session with HR and Team Leads

## Where to Go

On your first day, head directly to the **Bellandur office reception** located on Outer Ring Road. The reception staff will guide you to the HR induction session.

## What to Bring

Please bring the following items on your first day:

1. **Government-issued ID** (for verification purposes)
2. **Any required documents** as specified in your offer letter (e.g., educational certificates, previous employment documents)
3. **Notebook and pen** for taking notes during the induction and meetings.

## HR Induction

The HR induction session will cover important topics such as:

- Overview of company policies and workplace standards
- Introduction to the Code of Conduct and core values (integrity, respect, excellence)
- Benefits and resources available to you
- Overview of the performance management process and learning opportunities
- Q&A session to address any immediate questions you may have.

## First-Week Checklist

To help you settle in, here’s a checklist for your first week:

1. **Complete all onboarding paperwork** in PeopleHub.
2. **Set up your Okta account** and enable MFA for secure access to company applications.
3. **Familiarize yourself with internal systems** like ServiceHub, PeopleHub, and the Password portal.
4. **Meet with your buddy** regularly to ask questions and get acclimated.
5. **Review the company’s Code of Conduct** and other relevant policies.
6. **Schedule one-on-one meetings** with your manager and team members to understand your role and expectations.
