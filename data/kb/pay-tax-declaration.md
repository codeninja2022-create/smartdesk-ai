# Tax Declaration and Investment Proofs

This article explains how to submit your income tax declaration (also called the investment declaration) at NovaTech Solutions, including the choice between the old and new tax regimes, the declaration deadline, proof submission guidelines, and details about Form 16. It also outlines common deductions available to employees.

## Old vs New Tax Regime

Employees can choose between the old and new tax regimes when making their investment declarations. Here are the key differences:

1. **Old Tax Regime**: 
   - Allows for various deductions and exemptions (e.g., Section 80C, HRA, etc.).
   - Higher tax rates but the ability to reduce taxable income through investments.

2. **New Tax Regime**:
   - Lower tax rates but fewer deductions and exemptions available.
   - Employees opting for this regime cannot claim most deductions available under the old regime.

Employees should evaluate their financial situation and potential deductions to choose the most beneficial regime.

## How to Submit Your Tax Declaration

Your tax declaration, also called the investment declaration, tells payroll which tax regime you have chosen and which investments you plan to make, so the right amount of tax is deducted from your salary each month. It must be submitted in PeopleHub by **30 April** for the financial year. Employees are encouraged to declare early to ensure accurate payroll processing and tax calculations.

To submit your tax declaration:

1. Log in to PeopleHub.
2. Navigate to the **Tax Declaration** section.
3. Choose the old or new tax regime.
4. Enter your planned eligible investments, such as contributions to EPF, PPF, life insurance premiums, ELSS and other specified investments.
5. Review the details and submit.

## Proof Submission

After declaring investments, employees must submit proof of these investments between **1 January and 31 January**. The process is as follows:

1. **Collect Proofs**: Gather all relevant documents such as receipts, certificates, and statements.
2. **Submission Process**:
   - Log in to PeopleHub.
   - Go to the **Expenses** section.
   - Select **Tax Proof Submission** and upload the documents.
   - Ensure all documents are clear and legible.

## Form 16

Form 16 is a certificate issued by the company that summarizes the salary paid and the tax deducted at source (TDS) during the financial year. Here’s what you need to know:

1. **Issuance**: Form 16 will be issued by **15 June** of each year and will be available in PeopleHub.
2. **Contents**: It includes details of salary, deductions, and TDS, which are essential for filing income tax returns.
3. **Accessing Form 16**:
   - Log in to PeopleHub.
   - Navigate to the **Payroll** section.
   - Download your Form 16 once it is available.

## Common Deductions

Employees can claim various deductions under the old tax regime, including:

1. **Section 80C**: Investments in PPF, ELSS, life insurance premiums, etc. (up to INR 1.5 lakh).
2. **Section 80D**: Health insurance premiums for self, spouse, children, and parents.
3. **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: For employees living in rented accommodation, subject to certain conditions.
4. **Standard Deduction**: A flat deduction of INR 50,000 on salary income.

It is important to keep track of eligible expenses and ensure proper documentation.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I submit my tax declaration?

Log in to PeopleHub, open the **Tax Declaration** section, choose your tax regime, enter your planned investments and submit by **30 April**.

### What is the deadline for submitting my tax declaration?

The tax declaration (investment declaration) must be submitted in PeopleHub by **30 April**.

### When can I submit proof of my investments?

Proof of investments can be submitted between **1 January and 31 January**.

### How do I access my Form 16?

You can access your Form 16 in PeopleHub under the **Payroll** section after it is issued by **15 June**.

### Can I switch between the old and new tax regimes every year?

Yes, you can choose between the old and new tax regimes each financial year when making your investment declaration.

### What are some common deductions I can claim?

Common deductions include those under Section 80C (e.g., PPF, ELSS), Section 80D (health insurance), and HRA for rented accommodation.
