# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) and Anti-Harassment

This article outlines the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) policy at NovaTech Solutions, detailing the definition of harassment, the role of the Internal Committee, how to file a complaint, timelines for the process, confidentiality measures, and the non-retaliation policy. It aims to foster a safe and respectful workplace for all employees.

## Definition of Harassment

Harassment, as defined under the POSH policy, includes any unwelcome act or behavior of a sexual nature that creates an intimidating, hostile, or humiliating environment for the victim. This can encompass physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct that is sexual in nature.

## Internal Committee (PCC)

The Internal Committee, also known as the Presiding Complaints Committee (PCC), is responsible for addressing complaints of sexual harassment. Its main responsibilities include:

1. Receiving and addressing complaints of sexual harassment.
2. Conducting inquiries in a confidential and unbiased manner.
3. Recommending appropriate action based on the findings of the inquiry.
4. Creating awareness by conducting training sessions on POSH.

## How to File a Complaint

To file a complaint regarding sexual harassment, follow these steps:

1. Prepare a written complaint detailing the incident(s), including dates, times, locations, and any witnesses.
2. Submit the complaint to the Internal Committee (PCC) via email at ic@novatech.example or through the designated reporting channel on the company intranet.
3. Ensure that your complaint is submitted within 3 months of the incident.

## Timelines

Once a complaint is received, the following timelines apply:

1. The PCC will acknowledge receipt of the complaint within 7 days.
2. An inquiry will be initiated within 10 days of acknowledgment.
3. The inquiry is expected to be completed within 30 days, and the final report will be submitted to the appropriate authority.

## Confidentiality

All complaints and inquiries will be handled with the utmost confidentiality. The identities of the complainant and the accused will be protected throughout the process, and information will only be shared with individuals directly involved in the inquiry.

## Non-Retaliation Policy

NovaTech Solutions has a strict non-retaliation policy. Employees who report harassment or participate in an investigation will not face any adverse consequences or retaliation. Any form of retaliation against individuals who report harassment is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action.
