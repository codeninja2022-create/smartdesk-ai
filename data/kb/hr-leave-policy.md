# Leave Policy

This article outlines the leave policy at NovaTech Solutions, detailing the entitlements, eligibility, approval processes, and rules regarding casual leave, sick leave, earned leave, maternity leave, paternity leave, and compensatory off.

## Casual Leave

1. **Entitlement**: Employees are entitled to 12 days of casual leave per year.
2. **Eligibility**: All employees are eligible for casual leave.
3. **Approval**: Casual leave must be applied for and approved in PeopleHub. A maximum of 3 consecutive days can be taken at once.
4. **Carry-Forward and Encashment**: Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and is not eligible for encashment.

## Sick Leave

1. **Entitlement**: Employees are entitled to 8 days of sick leave per year.
2. **Eligibility**: All employees are eligible for sick leave.
3. **Approval**: Sick leave must be applied for and approved in PeopleHub. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
4. **Carry-Forward and Encashment**: Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and is not eligible for encashment.

## Earned Leave

1. **Entitlement**: Employees are entitled to 15 days of earned leave per year, accruing at a rate of 1.25 days per month.
2. **Eligibility**: All employees are eligible for earned leave.
3. **Approval**: Earned leave must be applied for and approved in PeopleHub.
4. **Carry-Forward and Encashment**: Earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days and is eligible for encashment upon exit from the company.

## Maternity Leave

1. **Entitlement**: Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave.
2. **Eligibility**: All female employees are eligible for maternity leave.
3. **Approval**: Maternity leave must be applied for and approved in PeopleHub, with appropriate documentation.
4. **Carry-Forward and Encashment**: Maternity leave is not subject to carry-forward or encashment.

## Paternity Leave

1. **Entitlement**: Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paternity leave within 3 months of the birth or adoption of a child.
2. **Eligibility**: All male employees are eligible for paternity leave.
3. **Approval**: Paternity leave must be applied for and approved in PeopleHub.
4. **Carry-Forward and Encashment**: Paternity leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Compensatory Off

1. **Entitlement**: Employees are entitled to compensatory off for approved weekend or holiday work.
2. **Eligibility**: Compensatory off is available to employees who have worked on weekends or holidays with prior approval.
3. **Approval**: To apply for compensatory off, employees must first obtain email approval from their manager and then submit the request in the leave management system within 7 days of the extra work.
4. **Carry-Forward and Encashment**: Compensatory off must be availed within 60 days from the date it is accrued and cannot be carried forward or encashed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I avail compensatory off before it is approved in the leave management system?

No, you must wait for your compensatory off to be approved in the company’s leave management system before availing it. This ensures that your leave is properly documented and authorized.

### Is it possible to combine compensatory off with other types of leave?

Yes, you can combine compensatory off with other types of leave, but it cannot be clubbed with more than two types of leave at a time, such as casual leave or earned leave.

### What is the process for informing about planned and unplanned leaves in our company?

For planned leaves, you must inform the 'Leave Plans' channel at least a week in advance and obtain approval from your manager to ensure proper workload distribution. For unplanned sick leave, update the 'Leave Plans' channel between 9:30 to 10 AM or before your scrum, whichever is earlier.

### What happens if I forget to apply for compensatory off in the leave management system after getting email approval?

If you forget to apply for compensatory off in the leave management system after getting email approval, your leave will not be officially recorded or approved. It is essential to complete the process in the leave management system to ensure your compensatory off is recognized and can be availed.

### What is the process for applying for compensatory off in our company?

To apply for compensatory off, you must submit your request within 7 days of performing the extra work. This should be done using the company’s leave management system. The request will then be reviewed and is subject to approval by your reporting manager, based on business requirements and staffing needs.

### What is the policy for availing compensatory off in our company?

Compensatory off should be availed within 60 days from the date it is accrued. The request for compensatory off must be approved by your reporting manager and should not impact project deliverables. Unused compensatory off will not be carried forward beyond the 60-day period, and employees cannot encash compensatory off.

### What should an employee do if they need to take an unplanned leave due to a medical emergency?

In the event of an unplanned leave, such as a medical emergency, the employee should notify their manager as soon as possible, preferably via call or message. This ensures that the manager is aware of the absence and can make necessary arrangements.
