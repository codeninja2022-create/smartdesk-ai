# Reporting Phishing and Security Incidents

This article provides guidance on recognizing phishing attempts, reporting them using the Report Phishing button, and the necessary steps to take after clicking a suspicious link. It also covers how to report lost devices or security alerts to ensure the safety of our IT environment.

## Recognizing Phishing Attempts

Phishing emails often try to trick you into revealing personal information or clicking on malicious links. Here are some common signs of phishing:

1. **Suspicious Sender**: Check the sender's email address. Phishing emails may come from addresses that look similar to legitimate ones but have slight variations.
2. **Urgent Language**: Phishing messages often create a sense of urgency, prompting you to act quickly without thinking.
3. **Generic Greetings**: Be wary of emails that use generic greetings like "Dear Customer" instead of your name.
4. **Unexpected Attachments or Links**: Avoid clicking on links or downloading attachments from unknown or unexpected emails.
5. **Poor Grammar and Spelling**: Many phishing emails contain spelling mistakes or awkward phrasing.

## Using the Report Phishing Button

If you receive a suspicious email, you can report it using the Report Phishing button in Microsoft Outlook. Here’s how:

1. Open the suspicious email in Outlook.
2. Click on the **Home** tab in the ribbon.
3. Look for the **Report Message** group.
4. Click on the **Report Phishing** button.
5. Follow any prompts to confirm your report.

This will help our IT security team investigate the email and take necessary actions.

## What to Do After Clicking a Suspicious Link

If you accidentally click on a suspicious link, take the following steps immediately:

1. **Disconnect from the Internet**: This helps prevent any potential malware from communicating with external servers.
2. **Run a Security Scan**: Use your antivirus software to run a full system scan to check for malware.
3. **Change Your Passwords**: Change your passwords for any accounts that may have been compromised.
4. **Notify IT**: Report the incident to the IT Service Desk via ServiceHub or email itsupport@novatech.example. Provide details about the link you clicked and any unusual activity you noticed.

## Reporting Lost Devices or Security Alerts

If you lose a company device or notice any security alerts, please report it immediately:

1. **Lost Devices**: Contact the IT Service Desk at ext. 2020 or via ServiceHub. Provide details about the device, including the type, model, and last known location.
2. **Security Alerts**: If you receive alerts about unauthorized access or suspicious activity, report these incidents through ServiceHub or email itsupport@novatech.example. Include any relevant details to assist the investigation.

Look for suspicious sender addresses, urgent language, generic greetings, unexpected attachments or links, and poor grammar or spelling.

### What should I do if I receive a phishing email?

Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook to report the email. If you clicked a link, disconnect from the internet, run a security scan, change your passwords, and notify IT.

### How do I report a lost company device?

Contact the IT Service Desk at ext. 2020 or via ServiceHub, providing details about the lost device.

### What steps should I take if I notice unusual activity on my account?
