# Performance Review Cycle

This article outlines the performance review cycle at NovaTech Solutions, detailing the half-yearly review process, goal setting, self and manager reviews, ratings, calibration, increments, and performance improvement plans. Understanding this cycle is essential for employees to align their performance with company objectives and achieve personal growth.

## Half-Yearly Review Cycle

At NovaTech Solutions, performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October. This half-yearly cycle allows for regular feedback and alignment with organizational goals.

## Goal Setting

1. Goals are set at the beginning of each review cycle in PeopleHub.
2. Employees should collaborate with their managers to establish clear, measurable objectives that align with departmental and company goals.
3. Goals should be specific, achievable, and time-bound to facilitate effective evaluation.

## Self and Manager Reviews

1. Employees are required to complete a self-review in PeopleHub prior to the manager review.
2. The self-review should reflect on achievements, challenges, and areas for improvement.
3. Managers will then conduct their reviews, assessing employee performance against the established goals and providing constructive feedback.

## Ratings

1. Performance is rated on a 5-point scale:
   - 1: Unsatisfactory
   - 2: Needs Improvement
   - 3: Meets Expectations
   - 4: Exceeds Expectations
   - 5: Outstanding
2. Ratings are based on various criteria, including quality of work, adherence to timelines, teamwork, communication, and initiative.

## Calibration

1. Calibration sessions are conducted post-review to ensure consistency and fairness in ratings across teams.
2. Managers discuss employee performance and ratings to align on standards and address any discrepancies.

## Increments

1. Annual increments are effective from 1 April, based on performance ratings from the previous review cycle.
2. Employees rated 4 or 5 may be eligible for higher increments, while those rated 1 or 2 may not receive an increment.

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

1. If an employee receives a rating of 1 or 2, a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may be initiated.
2. The PIP outlines specific areas for improvement, measurable goals, and a timeline for reassessment.
3. Employees on a PIP will receive additional support and regular check-ins from their managers.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often will performance reviews be conducted for remote employees?

Performance reviews for remote employees will be conducted regularly, following the same half-yearly schedule as in-office employees, ensuring ongoing productivity and alignment with organizational goals.

### What criteria are used to evaluate employee performance in our company?

Employee performance is evaluated based on ownership, adherence to timelines, quality of work, initiative, teamwork, upskilling efforts, communication, punctuality, and acceptance of feedback.

### What should I do if I disagree with my performance rating?

If you disagree with your performance rating, discuss your concerns with your manager during the review meeting. You may also request a follow-up meeting to clarify any misunderstandings.

### Can I change my goals after they have been set?

Goals can be adjusted if necessary. Discuss any changes with your manager to ensure alignment with overall objectives and document the updates in PeopleHub.

### What happens if I am placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)?

If placed on a PIP, you will work with your manager to identify specific areas for improvement, set measurable goals, and establish a timeline for reassessment.
