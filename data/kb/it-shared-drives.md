# Accessing Shared Drives and SharePoint

This article provides guidance on accessing shared drives and SharePoint sites at NovaTech Solutions. It includes steps for requesting access, troubleshooting permission errors, obtaining owner approval, and mapping drives.

## Requesting Access to SharePoint Sites and Network Shares

1. **Identify the Site or Drive**: Determine the specific SharePoint site or network share you need access to.
2. **Contact the Owner**: Reach out to the owner or administrator of the SharePoint site or network share to request access. You can find the owner’s contact information in the site details or by asking your manager.
3. **Submit a Request**: If the owner requires formal documentation, submit a request via ServiceHub under the IT category, specifying the site/drive name and your reason for access.
4. **Wait for Approval**: The owner will review your request and grant access if appropriate. You will receive a notification once access is granted.

## Troubleshooting Permission Errors

If you encounter permission errors while accessing shared drives or SharePoint sites, follow these steps:
1. **Verify Permissions**: Check with the site or drive owner to confirm that you have the correct permissions.
2. **Check Mapping**: Ensure that the drive is correctly mapped on your computer. You can do this by:
   - Opening "This PC" on Windows or "Finder" on macOS.
   - Confirming that the drive appears in the list of available drives.
3. **Reconnect to the Drive**: If the drive is not visible, try remapping it:
   - For Windows: Right-click on "This PC" > "Map network drive" > Select a drive letter and enter the folder path.
   - For macOS: In Finder, select "Go" > "Connect to Server" > Enter the server address and click "Connect."
4. **Contact IT Support**: If the issue persists, submit a ticket via ServiceHub or email itsupport@novatech.example for further assistance.

## Obtaining Owner Approval

To obtain owner approval for access to a shared drive or SharePoint site:
1. **Identify the Owner**: Find out who the owner or administrator is for the site or drive.
2. **Draft a Request**: Write a clear request outlining your need for access, including any relevant project details.
3. **Send the Request**: Email the owner directly or use the appropriate communication channel as per your team’s protocol.
4. **Follow Up**: If you do not receive a response within a few days, consider following up politely to check on the status of your request.

## Mapping Drives

To map a shared network drive on your company laptop, follow these steps:
1. **Open File Explorer** (Windows) or **Finder** (macOS).
2. **For Windows**:
   - Click on "This PC".
   - Click on the "Computer" tab and select "Map network drive".
   - Choose a drive letter and enter the folder path (e.g., \\server\sharedfolder).
   - Check "Reconnect at sign-in" if you want it to be persistent.
   - Click "Finish".
3. **For macOS**:
   - In Finder, click on "Go" in the menu bar and select "Connect to Server".
   - Enter the server address (e.g., smb://server/sharedfolder) and click "Connect".
   - Select the shared drive and click "OK".
4. **Access the Drive**: Once mapped, the drive will appear in your File Explorer or Finder for easy access.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I do if I can't access a specific folder on the shared network drive?

If you're unable to access a specific folder, first verify your permissions with the folder owner. If you have the correct permissions, try remapping the drive. If the issue persists, submit a ticket to IT Support via ServiceHub.

### How do I request access to a SharePoint site?

To request access to a SharePoint site, identify the site owner, draft a request outlining your need for access, and send it directly to the owner or submit a request through ServiceHub.

### What does it mean if I receive a 'permission denied' error?

A 'permission denied' error typically indicates that you do not have the necessary permissions to access the requested resource. Check with the owner to confirm your access rights.

### Can I map a shared drive on my personal desktop?

Yes, you can map a shared drive on your personal desktop as long as you have the necessary permissions and are connected to the VPN if accessing from outside the office.

### How do I troubleshoot access issues with shared drives?

To troubleshoot access issues, verify your permissions, check the drive mapping, and ensure your network connection is stable. If problems persist, contact IT Support for assistance.
