# Buddy Programme

The Buddy Programme at NovaTech Solutions is designed to help new employees integrate smoothly into the company culture and their roles. Each new joiner is paired with a buddy for the first 90 days, providing support, guidance, and a friendly point of contact for any questions or concerns.

## What a Buddy Does

A buddy at NovaTech Solutions plays a crucial role in helping new employees acclimate to the workplace. Responsibilities include:

1. **Orientation**: Introducing the new joiner to the office layout, key policies, and procedures.
2. **Support**: Providing assistance with navigating internal systems such as PeopleHub and ServiceHub.
3. **Social Integration**: Helping the new employee connect with colleagues and participate in team activities.
4. **Feedback**: Offering constructive feedback and sharing experiences to help the new joiner understand the company culture.

## How Buddies Are Assigned

Buddies are assigned to new employees as part of the onboarding process. The assignment process includes:

1. **Selection**: HR identifies suitable buddies based on their experience, role, and willingness to help.
2. **Matching**: New joiners are paired with buddies from similar departments or roles to ensure relevant support.
3. **Notification**: Both the new joiner and the assigned buddy are notified prior to the new employee's start date, allowing them to connect before the first day.

## 90-Day Check-Ins

To ensure a successful integration, the following check-ins are conducted:

1. **Initial Check-In**: A week after the new joiner's start date, the buddy and the new employee meet to discuss any immediate questions or concerns.
2. **Midway Check-In**: At the 45-day mark, a more in-depth discussion occurs to assess the new joiner's comfort level and address any ongoing challenges.
3. **Final Check-In**: At the end of the 90 days, a final review is conducted to evaluate the new joiner's integration and gather feedback on the buddy experience.

## Being a Buddy

If you are selected to be a buddy, here are some tips to make the experience valuable:

1. **Be Approachable**: Create an open environment where the new joiner feels comfortable asking questions.
2. **Share Knowledge**: Provide insights about your role, team dynamics, and company culture.
3. **Encourage Participation**: Motivate the new joiner to engage in team activities and meetings.
4. **Be Patient**: Understand that adjusting to a new workplace can be challenging and offer support throughout the process.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I do if I have questions about my role during the buddy period?

Feel free to reach out to your assigned buddy for guidance. They are there to help you navigate any questions or concerns related to your role.

### Can I request a different buddy if I feel my assigned buddy is not a good fit?

Yes, if you feel that your assigned buddy is not a good fit, please contact HR at hr@novatech.example or ext. 3030 to discuss your concerns.

### What happens after the 90-day buddy period ends?

After the 90-day period, you are encouraged to maintain the relationship with your buddy, but formal check-ins will no longer be scheduled. You can continue to reach out for support as needed.

### How can I become a buddy?

If you are interested in becoming a buddy, express your interest to your manager or HR. They will consider your experience and willingness to support new joiners.
