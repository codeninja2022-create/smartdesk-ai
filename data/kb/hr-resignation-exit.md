# Resignation, Notice Period and Exit

This article provides a comprehensive overview of the resignation process at NovaTech Solutions, including how to submit your resignation in PeopleHub, the notice period requirements, asset return procedures, and details about your full and final settlement. It also outlines the exit checklist and the process for obtaining your relieving letter.

## Resigning in PeopleHub

To formally resign from your position at NovaTech Solutions, please follow these steps:

1. Log in to PeopleHub.
2. Navigate to the ‘Leave’ section.
3. Select ‘Resignation’ from the options available.
4. Fill out the resignation form, including your last working day based on your notice period.
5. Submit the form for approval from your manager.
6. Once approved, you will receive a confirmation of your resignation.

## Notice Period

The notice period at NovaTech Solutions is as follows:
- **During Probation**: 30 days.
- **Post-Confirmation**: 60 days.

Your last working day will be calculated based on the notice period you provide in your resignation. Ensure that you adhere to this notice period to avoid any complications with your final settlement.

## Buyout of Notice Period

If you wish to leave before completing your notice period, you may request a buyout. The following steps should be taken:

1. Discuss your intention with your manager.
2. Submit a formal request for a buyout in PeopleHub.
3. Await approval from your manager and HR.
4. If approved, you will need to pay the equivalent of your notice period salary as per the company policy.

## Exit Checklist

Before your last working day, ensure you complete the following:
1. Submit all pending leave requests in PeopleHub.
2. Complete the exit interview process with HR.
3. Ensure all tasks and responsibilities are handed over appropriately.
4. Prepare a list of company assets in your possession.

## Asset Return

All company assets must be returned by your last working day. This includes:
- Laptop
- ID badge
- Any other equipment issued to you.

Failure to return these assets may result in deductions from your final settlement or legal action. Please coordinate with your manager or the IT department for the return process.

## Full and Final Settlement

Your full and final settlement will include:
- Payment for any unused earned leave.
- Reimbursement of any pending expenses submitted in PeopleHub.
- Deductions for any unreturned company assets.

This settlement will be processed in the next payroll cycle following your last working day.

## Relieving Letter

To obtain your relieving letter:
1. Ensure all company assets are returned.
2. Complete the exit interview and checklist.
3. Submit a request for the relieving letter in PeopleHub.

The relieving letter will be issued after your final settlement is processed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a deadline for returning company assets after my employment ends?

Yes, company assets must be returned by your last working day as part of the exit process.

### What happens if I don't return company assets after leaving my job?

If you fail to return company assets, the company may deduct the value of the unreturned assets from your final settlement or take legal action.

### Can I change my resignation notice period after submitting it?

Once submitted, your notice period cannot be changed without approval from your manager and HR.

### What should I do if I have pending expenses before my last working day?

Ensure you submit all pending expenses in PeopleHub within 30 days of your last working day to be included in your final settlement.

### How long does it take to receive my relieving letter after I leave?

The relieving letter will be issued after your final settlement is processed, typically within the next payroll cycle.
