# Microsoft Teams Meetings and Audio/Video

This article provides guidance on joining and hosting Microsoft Teams meetings, troubleshooting common audio and video issues, and maintaining proper meeting etiquette. It aims to ensure a smooth and professional experience for all participants.

## Joining and Hosting Teams Meetings

1. **Joining a Meeting:**  
   a. Open Microsoft Teams.  
   b. Navigate to the **Calendar** tab on the left sidebar.  
   c. Find the meeting you wish to join and click on it.  
   d. Click the **Join** button.  
   e. Choose your audio and video settings before entering the meeting.  

2. **Hosting a Meeting:**  
   a. Open Microsoft Teams and go to the **Calendar** tab.  
   b. Click on **New Meeting**.  
   c. Fill in the meeting details: title, date, time, and attendees.  
   d. Set the meeting to repeat if necessary, and add a description.  
   e. Click **Save** to send out invites.

## Troubleshooting Audio/Video Issues

1. **Microphone Issues:**  
   a. Ensure your microphone is connected and not muted.  
   b. In Teams, go to **Settings** > **Devices**.  
   c. Select your microphone from the dropdown menu.  
   d. Test your microphone by speaking and checking the input level.  

2. **Camera Issues:**  
   a. Check if your camera is connected and not blocked.  
   b. In Teams, go to **Settings** > **Devices**.  
   c. Select your camera from the dropdown menu.  
   d. Test your camera in a meeting or by using the **Make a test call** feature.  

3. **Screen Sharing Issues:**  
   a. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to share your screen.  
   b. Click on the **Share** button during a meeting and select the screen or window you want to share.  
   c. If screen sharing fails, check your network bandwidth and application settings.  
   d. Restart Teams if the issue persists.

## Meeting Room Etiquette

1. **Before the Meeting:**  
   a. Test your audio and video equipment beforehand.  
   b. Ensure you are in a quiet, well-lit space.  
   c. Join the meeting a few minutes early to address any technical issues.  

2. **During the Meeting:**  
   a. Mute your microphone when not speaking to minimize background noise.  
   b. Use the video feature to engage with participants, if possible.  
   c. Avoid multitasking; stay focused on the meeting.  
   d. Use the chat feature for questions or comments without interrupting the speaker.  

3. **After the Meeting:**  
   a. Follow up with any action items discussed.  
   b. Provide feedback if necessary to improve future meetings.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I join a Microsoft Teams meeting?

To join a Microsoft Teams meeting, open Teams, go to the Calendar tab, find the meeting, and click the Join button.

### What should I do if my microphone is not working during a meeting?

Check that your microphone is connected and not muted. Go to Settings > Devices in Teams to select the correct microphone and test it.

### How can I share my screen in a Teams meeting?

During a meeting, click on the Share button and select the screen or window you want to share. Ensure you have the necessary permissions.

### What are the best practices for meeting room etiquette?

Join meetings early, mute your microphone when not speaking, use video if possible, and avoid multitasking.

### What should I do if I experience video or audio desynchronization?

Check your network bandwidth and latency. Restart Teams and ensure your audio and video devices are functioning properly.
