# Connecting to Corporate Wi-Fi

This article provides step-by-step instructions for connecting to the corporate Wi-Fi network, NovaTech-Corp, using Okta credentials on both laptops and smartphones. It also addresses common issues such as certificate prompts and troubleshooting failed connections.

## Connecting to NovaTech-Corp Wi-Fi

To connect to the corporate Wi-Fi network, follow these steps:

### For Laptops:
1. **Select the Wi-Fi Network:**
   - Click on the Wi-Fi icon in the system tray (Windows) or menu bar (Mac).
   - Select **NovaTech-Corp** from the list of available networks.

2. **Enter Okta Credentials:**
   - When prompted, enter your Okta username and password.
   - If you have Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) enabled, complete the MFA process using Okta Verify or your hardware security key.

3. **Accept Security Certificates:**
   - If you receive a prompt regarding security certificates, ensure you accept the certificate to establish a secure connection.

4. **Verify Connection:**
   - Once connected, you should see a confirmation message. You can check your connection status in the network settings.

## Connecting to NovaTech-Corp Wi-Fi on Smartphones

To connect to the corporate Wi-Fi network on your smartphone, follow these steps:

### For iOS Devices:
1. **Open Settings:**
   - Go to **Settings** > **Wi-Fi**.

2. **Select the Wi-Fi Network:**
   - Tap on **NovaTech-Corp**.

3. **Enter Okta Credentials:**
   - Enter your Okta username and password when prompted.
   - Complete the MFA process if required.

4. **Accept Security Certificates:**
   - If prompted, accept the security certificate to connect.

### For Android Devices:
1. **Open Settings:**
   - Go to **Settings** > **Network & internet** > **Wi-Fi**.

2. **Select the Wi-Fi Network:**
   - Tap on **NovaTech-Corp**.

3. **Enter Okta Credentials:**
   - Input your Okta username and password.
   - Complete the MFA process if prompted.

4. **Accept Security Certificates:**
   - Accept any security certificate prompts to establish a secure connection.

## Troubleshooting Failed Connections

If you are unable to connect to the NovaTech-Corp Wi-Fi network, try the following troubleshooting steps:

1. **Check Wi-Fi Settings:**
   - Ensure that Wi-Fi is enabled on your device and that you are attempting to connect to **NovaTech-Corp**.

2. **Restart Your Device:**
   - Sometimes a simple restart can resolve connectivity issues.

3. **Forget and Reconnect:**
   - On your device, forget the **NovaTech-Corp** network and try reconnecting by entering your credentials again.

4. **Check for Certificate Issues:**
   - If you encounter certificate prompts, ensure you are accepting the correct certificates. If unsure, contact IT support.

5. **Verify Okta Credentials:**
   - Double-check that you are entering the correct Okta username and password. Ensure that your account is not locked or disabled.

6. **Contact IT Support:**
   - If you continue to experience issues, please reach out to the IT Service Desk via ServiceHub or email itsupport@novatech.example for further assistance.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I do if I see a certificate error when connecting to NovaTech-Corp?

If you see a certificate error, ensure you are accepting the correct security certificate. If you are unsure, please contact IT support for guidance.

### Can I connect to NovaTech-Corp Wi-Fi from outside the office?

No, the NovaTech-Corp Wi-Fi network is only accessible from within the office premises. If you are working remotely, please use the VPN to access internal systems.

### What if my Okta credentials are not working when trying to connect to Wi-Fi?

Double-check that you are entering the correct username and password. If you continue to have issues, verify that your account is active and not locked. You can also reset your password via the Password portal if needed.

### How can I troubleshoot if my device keeps disconnecting from the Wi-Fi?

Try restarting your device, forgetting the network and reconnecting, and ensuring that your device's Wi-Fi settings are correct. If the problem persists, contact IT support for further assistance.

### Is there a limit to the number of devices I can connect to NovaTech-Corp Wi-Fi?

While there is no specific limit stated, it is advisable to connect only the devices you actively use to avoid network congestion.
