# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup

This article provides a comprehensive guide on setting up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) using Okta Verify for NovaTech Solutions employees. It covers enrollment, options for push notifications versus verification codes, and procedures for handling scenarios such as changing or losing your phone, as well as how to request an MFA reset from the IT Service Desk.

## Enrolling Okta Verify

To set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) using Okta Verify, follow these steps:

1. **Download Okta Verify**: Install the Okta Verify app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android).
2. **Log in to Okta**: Go to the Okta login page and enter your company credentials.
3. **MFA Setup Prompt**: After logging in, you will be prompted to set up MFA. Select 'Set up' next to Okta Verify.
4. **Open Okta Verify**: Open the Okta Verify app on your mobile device.
5. **Add Account**: Tap on 'Add Account' and choose 'Scan a QR Code'.
6. **Scan QR Code**: Use your phone to scan the QR code displayed on your computer screen.
7. **Complete Setup**: After scanning, you will see your account added in the app. Click 'Next' on your computer to complete the setup.

## Push Notifications vs. Verification Codes

When using Okta Verify, you can choose between two methods for MFA:

- **Push Notifications**: This is the preferred method. When you log in, you will receive a push notification to your mobile device. Simply approve the request to complete the login.
- **Verification Codes**: If you do not receive push notifications, you can opt to receive a 6-digit verification code instead. This code is generated by the Okta Verify app. Enter the code when prompted during the login process.

## New Phone or Lost Phone

If you get a new phone or lose your current phone, follow these steps:

1. **New Phone**:
   - Install Okta Verify on your new device.
   - Log in to Okta on your computer and go to your account settings.
   - Remove the old device from your MFA settings.
   - Follow the enrollment steps to add your new device.

2. **Lost Phone**:
   - Immediately contact the IT Service Desk via ServiceHub or email itsupport@novatech.example.
   - Request an MFA reset to remove the lost device from your account.
   - Once the reset is completed, you can set up Okta Verify on a new device.

## MFA Reset by the Service Desk

If you are unable to access your MFA due to issues with your device or app, you can request an MFA reset from the IT Service Desk:

1. **Contact IT Service Desk**: Reach out via ServiceHub or email itsupport@novatech.example.
2. **Provide Details**: Include your employee ID and explain the issue you are facing.
3. **Wait for Confirmation**: The IT team will process your request and notify you once the reset is complete.
4. **Set Up MFA Again**: After receiving confirmation, follow the enrollment steps to set up MFA again using a new device or app.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I set up Multi-Factor Authentication for my company email account?

To set up Multi-Factor Authentication for your company email, follow the steps in the 'Enrolling Okta Verify' section above. This will enable MFA for all applications linked to your Okta account, including email.

### What should I do if I can't receive verification codes for MFA?

If you are not receiving verification codes, ensure that your mobile device has a stable internet connection. If using SMS, check with your carrier for any delivery issues. If the problem persists, contact the IT Service Desk for assistance.

### Can I use a different authentication app instead of Okta Verify?

No, NovaTech Solutions requires the use of Okta Verify for Multi-Factor Authentication to ensure compliance with corporate security policies.

### What happens if I change my phone number?

If you change your phone number, you will need to reset your MFA settings. Contact the IT Service Desk to request an MFA reset and then set up Okta Verify on your new device.

### How do I request an MFA reset?

To request an MFA reset, contact the IT Service Desk via ServiceHub or email itsupport@novatech.example. Provide your employee ID and details of the issue you're facing.
