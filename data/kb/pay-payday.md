# Pay-Day Schedule

This article outlines the pay-day schedule at NovaTech Solutions, including when salaries are paid, considerations for holidays and weekends, procedures for addressing late salary payments, and how to change your salary bank account details.

## Salary Payment Schedule

Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. You can expect your payslip to be available in PeopleHub by the 1st of the following month.

## Holidays and Weekends Around Pay Day

If the last working day of the month falls on a holiday or weekend, the salary will still be processed on that day. However, please note that if the last working day is a holiday, the payslip will still be available in PeopleHub on the next working day.

## What to Do If Your Salary Is Late

If you do not receive your salary on the scheduled pay day, follow these steps:

1. **Check PeopleHub**: Log into PeopleHub to confirm that your payslip has been generated.
2. **Verify Bank Account Details**: Ensure that your salary bank account details are correct in PeopleHub.
3. **Contact Payroll Team**: If everything appears correct and your salary is still not credited, reach out to the Payroll team at payroll@novatech.example for assistance.

## Changing Your Salary Bank Account

To change your salary bank account, follow these steps:

1. **Log into PeopleHub**: Access your account using your Okta credentials.
2. **Navigate to Bank Details**: Go to the 'Personal Information' section and select 'Bank Details'.
3. **Update Account Information**: Enter your new bank account details and ensure all information is accurate.
4. **Submit Changes**: Save your changes and submit them for approval.
5. **Confirmation**: You will receive a confirmation once your request has been processed.

## Frequently asked questions

### When will I receive my payslip?

You will receive your payslip in PeopleHub by the 1st of the next month after your salary is paid.

### What should I do if my salary is not credited to my account?

Check PeopleHub for your payslip, verify your bank account details, and contact the Payroll team at payroll@novatech.example if the issue persists.

### Can I change my salary bank account details?

Yes, you can change your salary bank account details in PeopleHub under the 'Personal Information' section.

### How is overtime calculated for weekend work?

Overtime for work on weekends is calculated separately from regular overtime and is subject to prior approval. Please refer to the company's specific overtime calculation rules.
