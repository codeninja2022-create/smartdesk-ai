# Grievance Redressal and Whistleblower Policy

This article outlines the Grievance Redressal and Whistleblower Policy at NovaTech Solutions, detailing the procedures for raising workplace grievances, the escalation process, and the protections in place for whistleblowers. It aims to foster a safe environment for reporting misconduct and ensuring that all concerns are addressed appropriately.

## Raising Workplace Grievances

Employees are encouraged to raise any workplace grievances promptly and through the appropriate channels. To raise a grievance, follow these steps:

1. **Identify the Issue**: Clearly define the nature of your grievance, whether it relates to workplace conduct, policy violations, or other concerns.
2. **Initial Discussion**: If comfortable, discuss the issue directly with the person involved or your immediate supervisor. This can often lead to a quick resolution.
3. **Formal Submission**: If the issue is not resolved, submit a formal grievance through the ServiceHub under the HR category. Include all relevant details and any supporting documentation.
4. **Acknowledgment**: You will receive an acknowledgment of your grievance within 5 working days, outlining the next steps.

## Escalation Levels

If your grievance is not resolved satisfactorily, you may escalate it as follows:

1. **Direct Supervisor**: If your grievance involves your supervisor, escalate to their manager.
2. **HR Help Desk**: Contact the HR Help Desk via ServiceHub or email hr@novatech.example for further assistance.
3. **Internal Committee (POSH)**: For grievances related to harassment or discrimination, you may contact the Internal Committee at ic@novatech.example.
4. **Ethics and Whistleblower Line**: For serious violations or if you feel uncomfortable with the above steps, you can report to the ethics line at ethics@novatech.example.

## Anonymous Whistleblowing

NovaTech Solutions encourages employees to report any known or suspected violations of laws, regulations, or company policies. To report anonymously:

1. **Use the Whistleblower Mechanism**: Submit your report through the designated anonymous reporting channel, which can be accessed via the HR section in ServiceHub.
2. **Confidentiality Assurance**: All reports will be treated confidentially, and your identity will be protected to the extent possible during the investigation process.

## Protection from Retaliation

The Whistleblower Policy at NovaTech Solutions includes several protections for individuals who report violations:

1. **No Retaliation**: Employees who report in good faith are protected from retaliation, including termination, demotion, or harassment.
2. **Confidentiality**: The identity of the whistleblower will be kept confidential throughout the investigation process.
3. **Disciplinary Action for Misuse**: Anyone found to be abusing the whistleblower mechanism or making false allegations may face disciplinary action.
